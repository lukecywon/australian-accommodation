Chapter 06

Conclusion

Where demand is strongest

  • New South Wales leads domestic and international visitor nights.
  • Tasmania stands out for domestic visitor nights relative to its population.
  • Regional Australia leads capital cities across the four accommodation types compared.

Accommodation choices and recovery

  • Rented properties and friends' or relatives' homes account for about three quarters of international visitor nights.
  • International visitor nights increased in rented properties and declined at friends' or relatives' properties.
  • Nominal tourism spending recovered faster than hospitality business counts grew, relative to 2018-2019.
